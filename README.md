# Nahlam

Nahlam is an early proposal for an open civic movement through which Syrians can help shape how homes and cities are rebuilt.

## The idea

Nahlam begins with the belief that Syrians should have a direct role in deciding what is built, how projects are organized, who is selected to carry out the work, and why each decision is made.

The movement seeks to bring communities together with architects, urban planners, engineers, legal professionals, companies, civil society organizations, funders, public institutions, and investors. Its purpose is to develop a transparent, community-led approach to housing and urban development in Syria.

## What open means

Nahlam applies open-source principles to the way the movement is organized and its work is carried out.

Plans, decisions, budgets, selection processes, contracts, and progress should be documented publicly. People should be able to examine the work, question it, improve it, and reuse what they learn.

The process is open. Personal information is not.

## Current status

Nahlam is at the beginning. It is currently one person’s proposal.

There is no project, funding, government agreement, or announced partnership.

The first task is to explain the idea clearly and invite others to examine it before deciding what the movement should become.
