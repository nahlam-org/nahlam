An open civic movement in formation

Let us shape what is rebuilt.

Nahlam is an early proposal for an open civic movement through which Syrians can help shape how homes and cities are rebuilt.

In preparation

Nahlam is still at the beginning.There is no land, housing, funding, government agreement, or announced partnership.

Plans, decisions, budgets, contracts, and progress should be documented openly. Personal information should remain private.
